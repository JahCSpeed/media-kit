02 / Content

What I make

Short-form videos built around real builds and real hardware decisions, not unboxings for the sake of it. People watch because they're actually deciding what to buy.

The signature bit

Every video, I use a different object as my "microphone," like a stick of RAM, a wrench, a controller, whatever fits that video. A brand's product can be that prop: it's in-hand and on-screen for the entire video, not a 3-second cutaway or an end-card mention. It fits the format naturally too, since the product becomes part of the bit instead of getting shoehorned in.

PC BUILDS
Full and budget build videos, covering part selection, compatibility calls, and the tradeoffs, not just a timelapse.
HARDWARE
GPU/CPU/cooling breakdowns, comparisons, and honest first-impressions on new releases.
OPTIMIZATION
Settings, BIOS tweaks, and performance tuning that gets a viewer measurably better FPS or thermals.
TECH NEWS
Fast takes on industry announcements, launches, and the stuff PC people are actually arguing about.
