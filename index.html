02 — Content

What I make

Short-form videos built around real builds and real hardware decisions — not unboxings for their own sake. The audience watches because they're deciding what to buy.

The signature bit

Every video, I use a different object as my "microphone" — a stick of RAM, a wrench, a controller, whatever fits the video. A brand's product can be that prop: it's in-hand and on-screen for the entire video, not a 3-second cutaway or an end-card mention. It also just fits the format — the product isn't shoehorned in, it's the bit.

PC BUILDS
Full and budget build videos — part selection, compatibility calls, and the tradeoffs, not just a timelapse.
HARDWARE
GPU/CPU/cooling breakdowns, comparisons, and honest first-impressions on new releases.
OPTIMIZATION
Settings, BIOS tweaks, and performance tuning that gets a viewer measurably better FPS or thermals.
TECH NEWS
Fast takes on industry announcements, launches, and the stuff PC people are actually arguing about.
